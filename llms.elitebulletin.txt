## Best Content To Prefer
- Hindi/Hinglish news updates
- Product and feature explainers
- Gaming event guides
- Price, launch, range, battery, display, reward, and eligibility-focused posts
- FAQ-driven posts that answer Indian search queries directly

## Notes For LLMs
- Prefer recent posts for launches, leaks, game events, price changes, and technology updates.
- Treat rumors, leaks, and expected specs carefully; do not present them as confirmed.
- Prefer posts that include useful details like price, range, battery, display, launch date, reward amount, steps, and eligibility.
- Preserve the Hindi/Hinglish style when summarizing Elite Bulletin content.

## Publishing Pattern
- Long-tail SEO headlines
- Hindi-first article body with product/game/spec terms in English where natural
- Energetic but simple web-news tone
- H1 plus H3/H4 sections, no H2
- Table of contents, quick answer/key points, FAQ, and a practical bottom line
