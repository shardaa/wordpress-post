# Elite Bulletin Style Rules

Use these rules whenever `--site=elitebulletin` is selected.

## Language And Voice

- Write primarily in Hindi using Devanagari.
- Use natural Hinglish for product names, game names, specs, and common digital terms, for example: Free Fire, GTA 6, Redmi, EV, Battery, Display, Launch, Update, Rewards, Diamonds, Skin, Creator, AI.
- Keep the tone energetic, newsy, and mass-market, similar to Elite Bulletin's existing posts.
- Use simple Hindi that a broad Indian search audience can understand.
- Do not write in formal newspaper Hindi. Keep it conversational, clear, and web-friendly.
- Avoid English-only paragraphs unless quoting a product name, feature, or official term.

## Headline Style

- Headlines should be long-tail, clickable, and keyword-rich.
- Start with the main entity when possible: product, game, event, car, bike, yojana, company, or feature.
- Use curiosity and benefit language naturally, such as "शानदार", "दमदार", "बड़ा अपडेट", "तहलका", "मचा दी चर्चा", "नया मौका", "पूरी जानकारी", "जानिए".
- Include numbers when relevant: price, range, battery size, refresh rate, date, reward amount, eligibility, version, or mileage.
- Keep headlines exciting but accurate. Do not promise confirmed facts when the research only says leak, rumor, report, or expected.

## Article Structure

- Begin with the focus keyword or main entity followed by a colon, like "Redmi K100 Pro:" or "Free Fire Max:".
- Open with a broad emotional/news hook that explains why the topic is getting attention.
- Use short to medium paragraphs, but let each paragraph explain one complete idea.
- Use clear H3/H4 sections after the H1. Do not use H2.
- Include a table of contents near the top when the article has multiple sections.
- Include a "quick answer", "मुख्य बातें", or "क्या खास है" style section near the top.
- Include an FAQ section near the end when useful.
- End with a simple conclusion or bottom-line paragraph that summarizes why the update matters.

## SEO And Topic Rules

- Choose one clear focus keyword in Hinglish or Hindi/Hinglish form.
- Use the focus keyword in the SEO title, meta description, slug, opening paragraph, and some subheadings where natural.
- Cover related search intents: price, launch date, features, specs, range, battery, rewards, diamonds, how to get, eligibility, pros/concerns, and whether it is useful.
- Use semantic variations instead of repeating one phrase too much.
- Use approved internal links only when they fit naturally.
- Keep URL slugs short, readable, and mostly English transliteration/product terms.

## Accuracy Rules

- Clearly label uncertain information with "रिपोर्ट्स के मुताबिक", "लीक्स के अनुसार", "उम्मीद की जा रही है", "अगर यह जानकारी सही साबित होती है", or "फिलहाल आधिकारिक पुष्टि नहीं हुई है".
- Do not invent launch dates, prices, specs, rewards, eligibility, quotes, official statements, or availability.
- If the topic is a leak or rumor, keep the article framed as possibility, not confirmed news.
- For yojana topics, be extra careful: mention eligibility, official source, and verification steps only when supported by research.

## Category-Specific Style

- Technology/smartphones: explain battery, display, camera, processor, charging, design, expected price, launch possibility, and user benefit.
- Esports/gaming: explain event, rewards, diamonds, skins, missions, how players may participate, why gamers are excited, and safety around fake reward claims.
- Automobile: explain price, range/mileage, safety, design, features, EMI/value angle, family/user benefit, and India relevance.
- AI: explain what changed, who can use it, why it matters, and practical impact in simple Hindi.
- Yojana: explain benefit, who may qualify, documents/process if research supports it, and where readers should verify.

## Avoid

- Do not sound like a US news site.
- Do not overuse generic SEO filler.
- Do not make medical, financial, legal, or government claims without support.
- Do not add fake official links or fake source names.
- Do not copy Elite Bulletin or source text verbatim.
